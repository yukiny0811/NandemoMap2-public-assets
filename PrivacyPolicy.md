  # Privacy Policy

  This privacy policy applies to the Nandemo Map 2 app, also known as なんでもマップ２, hereby referred to
  as the "Application", for mobile devices. The Application was created by Nandemo Map 2 Engineering Team, hereby referred
  to as the "Service Provider", as a Commercial service. This service is provided "AS IS".

  ## Information Collection and Use

  The Application lets you register your favorite places on a map. Depending on how you use the
  Application, the Application may collect, process, store, and upload information such as place names, map
  coordinates, notes, tags, photos, videos, search terms, support messages, and other content that you
  choose to add or provide.

  The Application may use this information to provide map features, save and restore your data, sync or
  back up user-created content, improve the Application, analyze usage, diagnose issues, display
  advertisements, personalize advertisements where permitted, and measure advertising performance.

  ## Data the Application May Collect

  The Application, the Service Provider, and third-party partners may collect the following categories of
  information:

  - Location information, including precise location and approximate location.
  - User content, including photos, videos, map pins, place names, notes, tags, search history, customer
  support content, and other user-provided content.
  - Identifiers, including user IDs, device IDs, advertising identifiers, app instance identifiers,
  installation identifiers, and similar identifiers.
  - Usage data, including product interaction, advertising data, and other usage data.
  - Diagnostics, including crash data, performance data, and other diagnostic data.
  - Other information required for app functionality, analytics, advertising, diagnostics, fraud
  prevention, security, or service improvement.

  Some of this information may be linked to you or your device.

  ## Location Information

  With your permission, the Application may access precise location information to show your current
  position on the map, help you add places to your map, and save map pins with location information.

  If you choose to save or sync map pins, precise location information associated with those pins may be
  uploaded to Firebase or other backend services used by the Application.

  Approximate location information may be used for app functionality, analytics, advertising, ad
  measurement, fraud prevention, service quality, developer advertising or marketing, and service
  improvement.

  Precise location information is used for app functionality and related analytics. The Application does
  not use precise location information for third-party advertising or tracking unless this Privacy Policy
  is updated and any required consent is obtained.

  ## Photos, Videos, and User Content

  The Application may access your camera or selected photos when you choose to add a photographed place or
  attach photos or videos to a map pin.

  Photos, videos, map pins, place names, coordinates, notes, tags, and other user-created content may be
  stored on your device and may also be uploaded to Firebase Storage, Cloud Firestore, or other Firebase
  services in order to provide app functionality such as saving, restoring, syncing, or backing up your
  content.

  User-created content is used for app functionality, analytics, diagnostics, service improvement, and
  support. It is not used to target third-party advertisements based on the content of your photos, videos,
  notes, or map pins unless this Privacy Policy is updated and any required consent is obtained.

  You can manage camera, photo, and location access from your device settings.

  ## Search History

  The Application may process searches performed in the Application, such as place searches or map-related
  searches. Search history may be used for app functionality, analytics, service improvement, and
  diagnostics.

  Search history is not used for third-party advertising or tracking unless this Privacy Policy is updated
  and any required consent is obtained.

  ## Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

  The Application uses Firebase Analytics to collect usage logs and understand how users interact with the
  Application. This may include app interactions, session information, app version, device information,
  approximate location, app instance identifiers, product interaction data, and other usage data.

  The Application uses, or may use, Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob and related advertising
  partners may collect and process information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP
  address, approximate location, advertising data, product interaction data, performance data, diagnostics,
  and other information related to ad delivery, personalization, measurement, fraud prevention, and service
  improvement.

  The Application may use data for personalized advertising, non-personalized advertising, ad measurement,
  analytics, developer advertising or marketing, and advertising performance improvement. This may involve
  linking data collected from the Application with data from third-party apps, websites, or services for
  advertising or advertising measurement purposes.

  On iOS, the Application may request permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework before
  accessing the Identifier for Advertisers, also known as IDFA, or before tracking your activity across
  apps and websites owned by other companies. If you do not allow tracking, the Application may still show
  ads, but tracking-related advertising identifiers may be limited according to Apple policy and SDK
  behavior.

  ## Crash Reporting and Diagnostics

  The Application may use Firebase Crashlytics to collect crash reports and diagnostic information when the
  Application crashes or encounters an error. This may include stack traces, relevant application state,
  device and operating system information, app version, timestamps, crash identifiers, performance
  information, diagnostic logs, and logs related to user actions before a crash.

  Crash reports and diagnostics may be used to identify bugs, improve stability, analyze app quality,
  improve app functionality, and understand technical issues.

  ## Cloud Storage and Backend Services

  The Application may use Firebase services, including Firebase Storage and Cloud Firestore, to store user-
  created content and related data. This may include photos, videos, map pins, coordinates, notes, tags,
  search-related data, identifiers, and metadata necessary to operate the Application.

  Data uploaded to Firebase services may be stored on servers operated by Google or its service providers.
  The Service Provider uses these services to provide app functionality, maintain user content, improve
  reliability, and support future features.

  ## Third-Party Services

  The Application uses third-party services provided by Google, including Firebase and Google AdMob. These

  Third-party services used or planned for use include:

  - Firebase Analytics

  ## Data Retention

  User-created content may remain in the Application and in backend services until you delete it, request
  deletion where available, or the Service Provider deletes it according to operational needs.

  Uninstalling the Application may remove data stored locally on your device, but it may not automatically
  delete data that has already been uploaded to Firebase or other backend services.

  Analytics, advertising, tracking, and crash-reporting data may be retained by Google, Firebase, AdMob, or
  related third-party service providers according to their own retention policies.
