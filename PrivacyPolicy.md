# Privacy Policy

  This privacy policy applies to the Nandemo Map 2 app, also known as なんでもマップ２, hereby referred to as the "Application", for mobile devices. The Application was created by Yuki Kuwashima, hereby referred to as the "Service Provider", as a Commercial service. This service is provided "AS IS".

  ## Information Collection and Use

  The Application lets you register your favorite places on a map. Depending on how you use the Application, it may process information such as place names, map coordinates, notes, tags, photos, and other content that you choose to add.

  The Application also collects and uses analytics, diagnostics, advertising, and tracking-related information to provide, maintain, improve, analyze, advertise, and measure the Application.

  ## Location Information

  With your permission, the Application may access your device location to show your current position on the map or to help you add a place to your map.

  Location information may also be used in coarse or approximate form for analytics, advertising, ad measurement, fraud prevention, service quality, or feature improvement, depending on your permissions, device settings, and applicable law.

  ## Photos and Camera

  The Application may access your camera or selected photos when you choose to add a photographed place or attach photos to a map pin.

  The Application uses photos for the features you request, such as creating or displaying saved places. You can manage camera and photo access from your device settings.

  ## Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

  The Application uses Firebase Analytics to collect usage logs and understand how users interact with the Application. This may include app interactions, session information, app version, device information, approximate location, and app instance identifiers.

  The Application uses, or may use, Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob and related advertising partners may collect and process information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location, advertising data, product interaction data, performance data, diagnostics, and other
  information related to ad delivery and measurement.

  The Application may use data for personalized advertising, non-personalized advertising, ad measurement, analytics, and advertising performance improvement. This may involve linking data collected from the Application with data from third-party apps, websites, or services for advertising or advertising measurement
  purposes.

  On iOS, the Application may request permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework before accessing the Identifier for Advertisers, also known as IDFA, or before tracking your activity across apps and websites owned by other companies. If you do not allow tracking, the Application may still show ads, but
  advertising identifiers such as IDFA will not be sent in ad requests where Apple policy and SDK behavior prohibit it.

  ## Crash Reporting and Diagnostics

  The Application may use Firebase Crashlytics to collect crash reports and diagnostic information when the Application crashes or encounters an error. This may include stack traces, relevant application state, device and operating system information, app version, timestamps, crash identifiers, and logs related to user
  actions before a crash.

  This information is used to identify bugs, improve stability, understand how the Application is used, and improve the user experience.
